## Reusable editorial rules

1. State whether a sentence is a product description, a synthetic example, a dated reported result or a current document-publication observation.
2. Preserve no-guarantee language in every translation. Never remove limitations to make promotional copy shorter.
3. Distinguish hub/editorial localization from utility-interface language. Do not announce a translated product UI.
4. Treat Legal, Compliance & Care as documentation practices, not legal/clinical advice, certification, automatic consent determination or nine launched apps.
5. Keep the six historical roadmap proposals distinct from the three additional Batch 99 references. Neither is in this four-repository delivery scope.
6. Use ordinary documented destinations without raw evidence or diagnostic parameters in URLs. Link navigation is not shared state or automatic synchronization.
7. Label missing release targets using the portfolio evidence register. Do not fabricate documents or verification results to make a link appear complete.
8. Keep credentials, customer captures, private decisions and account receipts out of public Markdown. Default to synthetic, minimal, manually reviewed summaries.
9. Preserve dated reports and archives. Correct current navigation with clearly labeled references instead of silently rewriting historical observations.
10. Any later software, deployment or rendered QA work needs a separate authorization; this content cycle contains none.

This publication supplies complete content in all three languages, consistent section coverage, unchanged product names, explicit utility-interface notices and source-backed limitations. No source gap authorizes scope expansion, a new repository, fabricated evidence or automatic replay of a real action.
