## EN

### EN H00 — Routing, ownership, acknowledgment, and stop rules

Scope: one hub and three utilities in exactly four owned repositories. The hub content role routes a saved-stream question to a proposed Stream/support reviewer, retry uncertainty to a proposed Retry/reliability reviewer, and browser access/cookie questions to a proposed CORS/browser-integration reviewer. Mixed or unsupported questions return to the portfolio owner for scope clarification. The six utility-to-utility routes below are documentary recipes, not six apps or observed live integrations.

The portfolio owner designates actual people; these receiving roles are not yet assigned. The sender minimizes and manually reviews the summary. The receiver records acceptance for document review, requests clarification, or places a hold. Content operations maintains source/locale mappings; EN/ES/PT language and relevant domain reviewers assess wording; a channel owner separately approves any later distribution. No acknowledgment, reviewer, date, meeting, SLA, or notification is invented.

Private handoff record: request ID; H-ID and related B-ID; locale; source document/revision and actual commit; synthetic question; allowed minimum summary; supplied facts versus unknowns; sender/receiver identity and role; acknowledgment decision and UTC date; private evidence location if authorized; next action and owner. Use statuses Prepared → Awaiting receiver → Accepted for document review / Needs information / Hold → Closed with an actual note. These are manual record states, not an automatic workflow. Do not put private identities, service receipts, raw evidence, or customer data in public Markdown.

Every route requires a receiver acknowledgment of purpose, exact source revision, permitted summary, uncertainties, and next owner before being marked accepted. A link click is not acceptance. Use synthetic values or explicitly approved minimized summaries; never place captures, prompts, credentials, raw headers/cookies, real intent keys, or personal data in URLs. No automatic sanitizer, shared state, execution ledger, backend synchronization, monitoring, or production replay exists. Stop for sensitive evidence, unsupported claims, unknown authorization, source conflict, wrong destination, or missing recipient; record a hold privately and refer to the accountable owner. A missing acknowledgment leaves the item awaiting/held, without a promised response time.

### EN H01 — Stream → Retry

Trigger: missing termination, repeated intent, or uncertain outcome in a supported saved capture. Sender: proposed Stream/support role; receiver: proposed Retry/reliability role. Carry only reviewed adapter, event count, terminal state, finding codes, timestamp availability, and a synthetic intent label. Ask which retry/retention assumptions need discussion, not whether a mutation should be replayed. Receiver acknowledgment must preserve unknown server completion and name the reconciliation owner. Hold if adapter support or evidence permission is unclear. A terminal is not semantic correctness and unavailable timing is not zero latency.

### EN H02 — Stream → CORS

Trigger: framing cannot be assessed because browser access or credential/cookie conditions are unresolved. Sender: proposed Stream/support role; receiver: proposed CORS/browser-integration role. Carry only a synthetic origin/site relationship, method, credential mode, known policy findings, and known/unknown sending/completion; include adapter only if relevant. Receiver acknowledgment separates response access from cookie conditions and states which facts are missing. Hold raw response/header data or unsupported browser conclusions. A hidden response does not prove that the server performed no action.

### EN H03 — Retry → Stream

Trigger: repeated-intent or ambiguous-completion discussion needs structural evidence from an already approved saved capture. Sender: proposed Retry/reliability role; receiver: proposed Stream/support role. Carry synthetic intent label, modeled budgets/retention, approved modeled counts if available, known/unknown completion, adapter, and timestamp availability. Receiver acknowledgment identifies supported framing findings that could inform the discussion without claiming semantic correctness. Hold if no approved capture or supported adapter exists; do not create a live capture or execute captured arguments through this handoff. A model does not establish a provider contract.

### EN H04 — Retry → CORS

Trigger: browser failure may hide server success or a cookie-policy assumption affects uncertainty. Sender: proposed Retry/reliability role; receiver: proposed CORS/browser-integration role. Carry synthetic origin/site relationship, method, credentials, preflight finding if known, and known/unknown sending/completion, not real keys or payloads. Receiver acknowledgment distinguishes preflight, response access, cookie eligibility, and operation completion. Hold any suggestion to blindly reflect Origin or automatically repeat a mutation. Actual outcomes require separately authorized reconciliation before replay; jitter is not identity and rate limits are not modeled by Retry.

### EN H05 — CORS → Retry

Trigger: an unreadable response leaves operation completion unknown. Sender: proposed CORS/browser-integration role; receiver: proposed Retry/reliability role. Carry synthetic method, origin/site relationship, credential mode, policy findings, and known/unknown completion. Receiver acknowledgment keeps access failure separate from server effect, records modeled assumptions, and identifies the real-outcome reconciliation role. Hold if server execution is asserted without evidence or if automatic replay is proposed. CORS is not authentication, authorization, or consent; stable modeled keys do not guarantee exactly-once execution.

### EN H06 — CORS → Stream

Trigger: access and cookie questions have been separated, but framing of an approved saved response remains unclear. Sender: proposed CORS/browser-integration role; receiver: proposed Stream/support role. Carry only a minimized approved policy summary, supported adapter, finding codes if available, terminal uncertainty, and timestamp availability. Receiver acknowledgment names the structural question and unsupported or missing evidence. Hold if raw cookies/tokens are attached, the adapter is unsupported, or a fresh probe is requested. Inspecting saved structure does not prove browser conformance or semantic correctness.

### ES H01 — Stream → Retry

Activador: terminación ausente, intención repetida o resultado incierto en captura guardada compatible. Remitente: rol propuesto Stream/soporte; receptor: rol propuesto Retry/fiabilidad. Lleve solo adaptador, número de eventos, estado terminal, códigos de hallazgos, disponibilidad temporal y etiqueta sintética de intención revisados. Pregunte qué supuestos de reintento/retención deben analizarse, no si debe repetirse una mutación. La recepción debe conservar la finalización desconocida del servidor y nombrar responsable de conciliación. Pause si soporte del adaptador o permiso de evidencia no están claros. Un terminal no prueba corrección semántica y tiempo no disponible no significa latencia cero.

### ES H02 — Stream → CORS

Activador: no puede evaluarse la estructura porque acceso del navegador o condiciones de credenciales/cookies siguen abiertos. Remitente: rol propuesto Stream/soporte; receptor: rol propuesto CORS/integración del navegador. Lleve relación sintética origen/sitio, método, modo de credenciales, hallazgos de política conocidos y envío/finalización conocidos o desconocidos; incluya adaptador solo si corresponde. La recepción separa acceso a respuesta de condiciones de cookies e indica hechos faltantes. Pause datos brutos de respuesta/encabezados o conclusiones de navegador no respaldadas. Una respuesta oculta no demuestra ausencia de acción del servidor.

### ES H03 — Retry → Stream

Activador: analizar intención repetida o finalización ambigua requiere evidencia estructural de una captura guardada ya aprobada. Remitente: rol propuesto Retry/fiabilidad; receptor: rol propuesto Stream/soporte. Lleve etiqueta sintética, presupuestos/retención modelados, conteos modelados aprobados si existen, finalización conocida/desconocida, adaptador y disponibilidad temporal. La recepción identifica hallazgos compatibles que podrían orientar el análisis sin afirmar corrección semántica. Pause si no hay captura aprobada o adaptador compatible; esta transferencia no crea capturas en vivo ni ejecuta argumentos capturados. Un modelo no establece un contrato del proveedor.

### ES H04 — Retry → CORS

Activador: un fallo del navegador puede ocultar éxito del servidor o un supuesto de cookies afecta la incertidumbre. Remitente: rol propuesto Retry/fiabilidad; receptor: rol propuesto CORS/integración del navegador. Lleve relación sintética origen/sitio, método, credenciales, hallazgo de preflight si se conoce y envío/finalización conocidos o desconocidos, no claves ni payloads reales. La recepción distingue preflight, acceso a respuesta, elegibilidad de cookies y finalización de operación. Pause propuestas de reflejar Origin sin validación o repetir automáticamente mutaciones. Los resultados reales requieren conciliación autorizada por separado antes de repetir; jitter no es identidad y Retry no modela límites de tasa.

### ES H05 — CORS → Retry

Activador: una respuesta ilegible deja desconocida la finalización de operación. Remitente: rol propuesto CORS/integración del navegador; receptor: rol propuesto Retry/fiabilidad. Lleve método sintético, relación origen/sitio, modo de credenciales, hallazgos de política y finalización conocida/desconocida. La recepción separa fallo de acceso de efecto del servidor, registra supuestos modelados e identifica el rol de conciliación de resultados reales. Pause si se afirma ejecución sin evidencia o se propone repetición automática. CORS no es autenticación, autorización ni consentimiento; claves modeladas estables no garantizan ejecución exactamente una vez.

### ES H06 — CORS → Stream

Activador: se han separado acceso y cookies, pero la estructura de una respuesta guardada aprobada sigue incierta. Remitente: rol propuesto CORS/integración del navegador; receptor: rol propuesto Stream/soporte. Lleve solo resumen de política minimizado y aprobado, adaptador compatible, códigos de hallazgos si existen, incertidumbre terminal y disponibilidad temporal. La recepción nombra la pregunta estructural y la evidencia faltante o no compatible. Pause si hay cookies/tokens brutos adjuntos, adaptador no compatible o solicitud de sondeo nuevo. Examinar estructura guardada no prueba conformidad del navegador ni corrección semántica.
