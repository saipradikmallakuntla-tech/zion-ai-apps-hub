Legal, Compliance & Care

Three operational pillars for organizing evidence and human review around the existing app network. These are documentation practices, not nine new apps or automated compliance services.

Not legal, regulatory, clinical, or medical advice. No compliance certification, consent determination, security guarantee, or exactly-once guarantee. Accountable people must review decisions in their own context.

The three diagnostic interfaces and their technical documentation are currently English-only. Changing this showcase language does not translate the satellite apps.

Legal

Keep decisions and provenance reviewable.

  • AI governance documentation

    Record intended use, accountable owner, model and version, data categories, human decision points, known limitations, and approval or review dates.

  • Vendor-contract audit checklist

    Inventory the agreement version, service scope, data handling and retention terms, incident contacts, change notices, and unresolved questions for qualified reviewers. This checklist does not interpret or approve a contract.

  • IP and licensing attribution log

    Record each asset or dependency, source, author, version, stated license, required notices, and review owner. Preserve evidence and flag unknown permissions; do not infer rights or license compatibility.

Compliance

Verify boundaries; retain dated, scoped evidence.

  • PII and consent boundary verification

    Map personal-data categories, approved purpose and consent scope, destinations, access, retention, and deletion responsibilities. Record mismatches for privacy review; this page neither collects data nor verifies consent automatically.

  • CORS and cookie security hygiene

    Inspect synthetic or redacted headers and cookie attributes with CORS & Cookie Doctor. Record the supplied context, findings, and browser validation still needed. CORS is not authorization or consent; this offline model is not a privacy-compliance scanner.

    Open diagnostic: CORS & Cookie Doctor · Source and documentation

  • Retry and rate-limit safety log

    Record retry budgets, timeouts, backoff, observed rate-limit responses, key-retention windows, and side-effect risks. Use Agent Retry Safety Lab for bounded retry scenarios. Rate-limit observations must come from an approved environment: the lab is not a live rate-limit tester or monitor.

    Open diagnostic: Agent Retry Safety Lab · Source and documentation

Care

Prepare people to diagnose, escalate, and recover.

  • Streaming reliability diagnostics

    Inspect saved synthetic or redacted SSE/NDJSON with LLM Stream Inspector. Record framing errors, incomplete streams, adapter assumptions, and reproducible examples. It does not run live inference, monitor uptime, or establish semantic correctness.

    Open diagnostic: LLM Stream Inspector · Source and documentation

  • Human-in-the-loop escalation playbook

    Define stop conditions, the accountable reviewer and backup, handoff evidence, response expectations, and approval before resuming. Use manual review for ambiguous or consequential outcomes; no human support or escalation service is provided by this page.

  • Incident response readiness

    Maintain an incident owner, a redacted timeline, containment and rollback steps, communication responsibilities, recovery checks, and a next-review date. Exercise the playbook with synthetic tabletop scenarios; this is not a monitoring or emergency-response service.

Minimal operational evidence record

  1. Practice and purpose; accountable owner and human approval point.
  2. Evidence reference and revision; environment, assumptions, and UTC observation time.
  3. Finding, limitation, unresolved risk, escalation owner, and next action.

Use synthetic or redacted examples only. Never paste real personal data, client documents, prompts, credentials, tokens, or session cookies into examples or publish them in logs. Keep sensitive evidence in an approved internal system.

This static page does not generate, submit, store, or synchronize logs. Copy the record structure into your approved tracker. Navigation carries no diagnostic input; there are no analytics, cookies, persistent storage, uploads, or runtime API calls.
