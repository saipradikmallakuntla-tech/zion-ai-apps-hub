Saved means a person retains a reviewed Markdown record in an authorized private location. Optional team/commercial discussion is separate and does not establish an available offering, customer relationship, price or SLA.

### EN SH01 — Free self-serve discovery checklist

- State one documentation question, intended audience and desired decision; separate supplied facts from unknowns.
- Use SH02 to select the first repository by its documented purpose, not a similar product name. Read its README, NETWORK, limits and exact source revision.
- Choose a synthetic example or an explicitly approved, minimized summary. Do not paste live secrets, customer/personal data, raw prompts/captures, headers/cookies, production schemas or real event/intent keys into public records or links.
- Record assumptions and unsupported cases before drawing a conclusion. Reading this checklist requires no purchase, intake email, lead form or automated follow-up.
- Save the bounded question and references using SH03. A saved document is not a software result, proof of server completion or permission to execute/replay an action.
- Continue independently for ordinary discovery. If a real team decision or unresolved risk needs another person, use SH04/SH05 manually; absence of a receiver means awaiting/hold, not acceptance.

### EN SH04 — Receiver acknowledgment, holds and closure

Use the existing manual states: Prepared → Awaiting receiver → Accepted for document review / Needs information / Hold → Closed with an actual note. A real receiver must acknowledge purpose, exact revision, permitted summary, uncertainties and next owner before acceptance is recorded. A link click, an exported file or a proposed role is not acceptance. No receiver leaves the record awaiting/held; no notification or response deadline is implied.

Stop for sensitive evidence, unknown permission, missing source/receiver, unsupported claims, conflicting revisions or uncertain real-action outcomes. Refer scope conflicts to the portfolio owner, wording/localization to content operations and designated language reviewers, and relevant domain/privacy questions to designated reviewers. Roles still need actual people. Close only with the real outcome or hold reason, source, owner, next action and UTC date recorded privately. Changed sources, wording, notices or destinations reopen affected reviews. Preserve authentic historical evidence gaps rather than inventing passing checks.

### ES SH04 — Acuse del receptor, esperas y cierre

Use los estados manuales existentes: Preparado → Pendiente de receptor → Aceptado para revisión documental / Requiere información / En espera → Cerrado con una nota real. Detenga el proceso ante pruebas sensibles, permiso desconocido, fuente/receptor ausente, afirmaciones no admitidas, revisiones contradictorias o resultados inciertos de acciones reales. Remita conflictos de alcance al responsable de cartera, redacción/localización a operaciones de contenido y revisores de idioma designados, y cuestiones pertinentes de dominio/privacidad a revisores designados. Aún deben asignarse personas reales. Cierre solo con resultado o motivo de espera real, fuente, responsable, próxima acción y fecha UTC registrados en privado. Cambios de fuente, texto, avisos o destinos reabren revisiones afectadas. Conserve las lagunas históricas auténticas sin inventar comprobaciones aprobadas.
