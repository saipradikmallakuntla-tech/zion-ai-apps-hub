## EN

### EN B01 — Decision and audience

**One starting point. Clear responsibilities. A reviewed next step.** Zion AI Apps Hub is a documentation and discovery entry point for portfolio sponsors, enablement editors, support leads, and AI/browser-integration teams deciding which bounded diagnostic aid merits discussion. Adopt the hub as a shared orientation resource, not as an execution platform or evidence of production readiness. This brief covers one hub and three utilities in four owned repositories; wider-network references and six roadmap proposals do not expand that scope.

### EN B01 — Appropriate use and prerequisites

Use the hub to compare documented purposes, limitations, language boundaries, and manual routes: Stream for supported saved-stream structure, Retry for modeled uncertain outcomes, and CORS for browser response access versus cookie conditions. Before an adoption discussion, designate an accountable sponsor, an evidence-handling policy, and language/domain reviewers. Existing documentation describes static, browser-local utilities without mandatory accounts or API keys; this publication does not audit that behavior or establish an enterprise service agreement.

### EN B01 — Proposed adoption discussion

1. Choose one synthetic business-support question and identify the appropriate utility from its limitations, not its name alone.
2. Review the relevant brief with the sponsor and support/integration role; record assumptions and unavailable evidence privately.
3. Agree what a useful, bounded output would be and who receives it. Do not run a pilot, move customer data, or authorize production replay through this checklist.
4. Record proceed-to-review, changes-requested, or hold with an actual owner and next action. Dates remain unset until assigned; no meeting or campaign is scheduled.

### EN B01 — Handoff and documentary acceptance

Use the [handoff plan](CROSS_REPOSITORY_HANDOFF_PLAN.md) to route a minimized question. The proposed receiving role acknowledges the exact source revision, purpose, permitted summary, and unresolved questions in approved private tracking. Documentary acceptance means the audience can choose the right utility, explain its non-goals, identify a responsible recipient, and locate the relevant source and notice in each language. These are proposed review criteria, not completed tests or measured adoption outcomes. Navigation carries no diagnostic state; a link is not an upload or an acknowledgment.

### EN B01 — Limits, language, and evidence

All three utility interfaces remain English-only. Full EN/neutral-ES/PT editorial copy is not software localization or native-language certification. Nine Legal/Compliance/Care practices are documentation practices, not nine apps or professional advice. Keep credentials, personal data, customer captures, raw headers/cookies, and private decisions out of public Markdown and URLs. No automatic sanitization, monitoring, shared runtime, compliance/security certification, SLA, or exactly-once guarantee is supplied. Historical observations retain their original dates; Batch 22/99 receipt gaps and unfinished architecture evidence remain unresolved.

### EN B01 — Accountable next step

The portfolio owner designates reviewers; content operations maintains sources and B01 locale decisions; language and relevant domain reviewers remain to be assigned. Keep channel distribution on hold until actual required approvals and separate distribution authorization exist. A wording, source, notice, or destination change reopens affected gates. Next: [review portfolio status](PORTFOLIO_STATUS.md), [read Stream's brief](https://github.com/saipradikmallakuntla-tech/llm-stream-inspector/blob/main/ENTERPRISE_ADOPTION_BRIEF.md), [review Retry's brief](https://github.com/saipradikmallakuntla-tech/agent-retry-safety-lab/blob/main/ENTERPRISE_ADOPTION_BRIEF.md), or [review CORS's brief](https://github.com/saipradikmallakuntla-tech/cors-cookie-doctor/blob/main/ENTERPRISE_ADOPTION_BRIEF.md). Un siguiente paso revisado.** Zion AI Apps Hub es una entrada de documentación y descubrimiento para responsables de cartera, equipos de capacitación, líderes de soporte y equipos de integración de IA/navegadores que deciden qué ayuda de diagnóstico acotada merece evaluación. La documentación describe utilidades estáticas y locales al navegador sin cuentas ni claves API obligatorias; esta publicación no audita ese comportamiento ni establece un acuerdo de servicio empresarial.

### ES B01 — Conversación de adopción propuesta

3. Acuerde una salida útil y acotada y quién la recibirá. Esta lista no ejecuta un piloto, transfiere datos de clientes ni autoriza repeticiones en producción.
4. Registre pasar a revisión, cambios solicitados o pausa, con un responsable real y una siguiente acción. Las fechas quedan sin fijar hasta su asignación; no se programa ninguna reunión ni campaña.

### ES B01 — Transferencia y aceptación documental

La aceptación documental significa poder elegir la utilidad correcta, explicar sus exclusiones, identificar un receptor responsable y encontrar la fuente y el aviso pertinentes en cada idioma. Son criterios de revisión propuestos, no pruebas completadas ni resultados de adopción medidos. La navegación no transporta estado de diagnóstico; un enlace no es una carga ni una confirmación de recepción.
